Noor: Prayer Times & Quran · Noor Islamic Apps

You can ask us to delete your Noor account and the data linked to it at any time. You do not need the app installed.

How to request deletion

  1. Email gulfarazahmed08@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Noor account".
  2. Send it from the email address you use to sign in to Noor (or write that address in the message). If you signed in with Google or Apple, use that account's email.
  3. We confirm by reply and delete your account within 30 days.

Email a deletion request

What we delete

  • Your Noor account: email address, display name and sign-in records (Firebase Authentication).
  • Any data stored on our servers under your account.
  • The link between your account and app usage and crash data.

What we keep

  • Anonymous usage statistics and crash reports that no longer identify you, for up to 14 months (analytics) and 90 days (crash reports), after which they are deleted automatically.
  • Nothing else.

Data on your phone

Bookmarks, reading position, Learn progress, tasbeeh counts, the Ramadan log and downloaded recitations are kept only on your phone. To remove them, uninstall Noor or clear its storage in your phone's settings.
