Privacy Policy

Effective 17 November 2026 · Noor Islamic Apps

This policy explains what the Noor: Prayer Times & Quran app ("Noor", "the app") collects, why, and the choices you have. Noor is published by Noor Islamic Apps ("we", "us"). We do not sell your data and the app shows no ads.

1. The short version

  • Prayer times and the Qibla direction are calculated on your phone. Your location is not sent to our servers.
  • Your Quran bookmarks, reading position, lessons, streak, tasbeeh count and Ramadan log are stored on your phone.
  • If you create an account, we keep your email address and name so you can sign in.
  • We use Google Firebase to run sign-in, to count app usage and to find crashes.
  • You can delete your account and its data at any time: Delete account.

We never put your email, name or location into analytics or crash reports.

3. Data that stays on your phone

Prayer settings, azan sound choices, Quran bookmarks and last-read position, downloaded recitations, Learn progress (lessons, XP, streak), tasbeeh counts, adhkar progress and the Ramadan fasting log are saved only on your device. We cannot see them. They are removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

4. Permissions

  • Location: for prayer times and Qibla. Optional; you can enter the app without it.
  • Notifications and alarms: to play the azan at prayer times. Scheduled on your phone.
  • Camera: only for the optional augmented-reality Qibla view. Images are processed live and never recorded or uploaded.

5. Third-party services

  • Quran recitation streams from EveryAyah.com. Their server receives your IP address when audio plays or downloads.
  • Google Maps shows the Qibla map. Google receives the map area you view. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Firebase (Google LLC) as listed above. See Firebase privacy information.

6. How long we keep data

  • Account details: until you delete your account.
  • Analytics data: up to 14 months. Crash reports: up to 90 days.

7. Your choices and rights

You can use Noor as a guest without giving an email address. You can turn off location and notifications in your phone's settings at any time. You may ask us to access, correct or delete your data by emailing gulfarazahmed08@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days.

8. Children

Noor is made for a general audience, including families learning together. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 beyond what is described here. A parent or guardian can ask us to delete such information at the email above.

9. Security

Data sent to Firebase is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Your sign-in session is kept in the app's private storage on your phone, which other apps cannot read.

10. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
